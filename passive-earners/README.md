# Track A — Passive earners (bandwidth + CPU sharing)

This track exists to make sure you see actual money **every day, starting tomorrow**, while Track B (`username-api`) builds up RapidAPI ranking over weeks.

## What it is

You install 4-5 desktop apps on your existing PC. Each app pays you a few cents to a few dollars per day in exchange for sharing your idle internet bandwidth (and sometimes CPU). The bandwidth is resold as residential-proxy traffic to enterprise customers like ad-verification firms, market-research companies, and SEO tools.

You don't write code. You don't market anything. You install, sign in, and leave the apps running.

## Hard rules — read these before installing anything

1. **Do NOT run on a corporate or work network.** Most corporate ToS prohibit residential proxy software. You can get fired. Use this on personal home internet only.

2. **Do NOT run alongside a paid VPN** that has a "no commercial use of the IP" clause (NordVPN, ExpressVPN, most others). Your VPN provider may terminate your account.

3. **Do NOT use a VPS or seedbox** to "scale" this. Datacenter IPs earn ~0 because they're not residential — the value to buyers is *residential* traffic. People who try this hit the floor and feel deceived.

4. **Do NOT lie to family/roommates** about what these apps do. Your internet may briefly feel slow during heavy proxy traffic; tell the people who share the connection.

5. **Don't expect more than coffee money.** Plenty of YouTube videos promise $50/day passive — those rely on multi-IP setups that violate every app's ToS and get accounts banned. We're playing the legitimate game, on one home IP, for $1-$2/day.
